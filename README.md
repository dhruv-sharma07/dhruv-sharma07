## 🧠 How I Build (Engineering Tenets)

Principles distilled from hackathons, systems design, and production builds:

* **Evidence over eloquence:** A fluent LLM explanation without a verifiable source (exact statutory section, git SHA, or audit log) gets dropped, not shipped.
* **Evals before polish:** If accuracy and latency cannot be benchmarked against a verifiable baseline, aesthetic UI is premature.
* **Principled abstention:** In domain-specific systems (such as legal or financial triage), *"I need more information"* or explicit refusal is infinitely superior to a polished hallucination.
* **Integrate, don't just demo:** True engineering value lives in handling real constraints — database migrations, auth lifecycles, rate limits, schema validation, and error boundaries from day one.
* **Transparent boundaries:** Clear architectural limitations belong directly in the README, not hidden behind marketing copy.

## 🚀 Featured Builds & Systems

### ⚖️ [NyayaBot — India's AI Legal Advisor](https://github.com/dhruv-sharma07/NyayaBot)
> **Deterministic legal guidance across IPC, CrPC, BNS 2024 & 200+ Indian Statutory Acts**

* **The Problem:** Navigating India's legal framework is slow, expensive, and intimidating. Generic LLMs routinely hallucinate US/UK legal codes and fail to cross-reference the landmark 2024 transition from IPC/CrPC to the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS/BNSS).
* **The Architecture:**
  * **Domain-Specific Jurisprudence Engine:** Grounded exclusively on Indian statutory law to prevent cross-jurisdiction bleeding.
  * **Dual-Code Cross Mapping:** Translates legacy IPC/CrPC offenses to active BNS/BNSS statutory sections with cited punishment thresholds.
  * **Zero-Config Browser Runtime:** Client-side execution with zero external server dependencies, instant latency, and complete user privacy.
  * **Deterministic Action Routing:** Identifies and routes citizens directly to designated official grievance portals (e-Daakhil, NCH 1915, cybercrime.gov.in) with concrete step-by-step action plans.
* **Tech Stack:** `Python` `NLP` `Indian Jurisprudence (BNS/BNSS)` `Vanilla JS Web Engine` `HTML5/CSS3`
* **Artifacts:** [Source Code](https://github.com/dhruv-sharma07/NyayaBot) · [Solution Deck (PDF)](https://github.com/dhruv-sharma07/NyayaBot/blob/main/NyayaBot_Hackathon_Solution_Deck.pdf)
